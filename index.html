Founded in 2023, ZUVO was built by parents and software engineers determined to replace uncertainty with real-time clarity.

From A Parent's Morning Worry To India's Leading School Bus Platform

ZUVO was born when our founder spent 50 minutes waiting at a bus stop with his 6-year-old daughter during heavy rain, without any way to know whether the bus was stuck in traffic or had already passed.

That frustration sparked a mission: leverage consumer-grade GPS mapping technology with enterprise-grade school transport dispatch tools.

Today, ZUVO powers thousands of daily routes across 28 states, preventing roadside delays and providing parents and school administrators absolute peace of mind.

Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 of India.

Legal Directory

1. Terms of Service 2. Privacy Policy 3. Cookie Policy 4. DPDP & Child Safety 5. Limitation of Liability

1. Terms of Service

By accessing the ZUVO platform, mobile applications, or administrator console, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you are entering into this agreement on behalf of an educational institution, you warrant that you possess full administrative authorization to bind that entity.

Service Scope

ZUVO provides live vehicle telemetry, geofence trip management, driver status logging, and notifications. We guarantee 99.9% platform availability across all school operating days.

2. Privacy Policy & Telemetry Safeguards

ZUVO Technologies Pvt. Ltd. treats student and location telemetry with highest confidentiality. We never sell, monetize, or broker student travel information to commercial advertisers or third-party aggregators.

Data Handling Principles

  • Driver GPS telemetry is recorded only while an active trip session is initiated by the authenticated driver.
  • Student identity records are encrypted end-to-end using AES-256 standard protocols.
  • Historical trip route records are purged automatically after 90 days unless archived by the school.

3. Cookie Policy

We utilize essential authentication tokens to maintain verified sessions. Non-essential tracking scripts are never loaded without explicit institutional approval.

4. India DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

In accordance with Section 9 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023), special safeguards are enforced regarding children's data. Processing of minors' personal information is undertaken solely with verified institutional and parental authorization.

5. Limitation of Liability

ZUVO operates as an information technology telemetry assistant. Physical vehicle operation, traffic compliance, and onboard passenger discipline remain the sole responsibility of the respective school administration and fleet drivers.
